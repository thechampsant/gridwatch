Sites and connectors

Status is a state, not an event, so a quiet Available connector is fine. What ages is the vendor's last confirmation. A site not heard from for 5 minutes is shown as stale and left out of nearest-available.

Nearest available charger

Geodesic distance (PostGIS KNN). Counts only connectors that are Available, recently confirmed by the vendor and free of critical faults, inside your tenant, within 50 km.

Freshness: what the vendor feed allows

Bars show how old events already were when the vendor served the poll page (server_time minus event time), before GridWatch receives anything. The dark tick is the contract target.
